  <div class="section-label">01 / Professional</div>
  <div class="project-feature">
    <div class="project-feature-top"><span class="project-kicker">Target Tech · Compute platform</span><span class="project-year">01</span></div>
    <h2>Grove</h2>
    <p class="project-lede">A custom virtualization service for running application containers with less overhead, more performance, and a higher security baseline.</p>
    <div class="project-detail-grid">
      <div><h3>The challenge</h3><p>Make a consistent, secure runtime for workloads spread across private datacenters and an expanding fleet of edge clusters.</p></div>
      <div><h3>The approach</h3><p>Grove packages containers and sidecars into purpose-built VM images on a lightweight Linux distribution, giving teams a focused runtime instead of a full Kubernetes footprint.</p></div>
      <div><h3>The impact</h3><p>Grove replaced Kubernetes across hundreds of clusters and is on its way to supporting more than 1,800 edge clusters.</p></div>
    </div>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>Go</span><span>Linux</span><span>Virtualization</span><span>Edge</span></div>
  </div>

  <div class="project-feature project-feature-secondary">
    <div class="project-feature-top"><span class="project-kicker">Target Tech · Private cloud</span><span class="project-year">02</span></div>
    <h2>VMaaS</h2>
    <p class="project-lede">A vertical-scaling virtualization service that orchestrates VMware vSphere and oVirt environments through a single platform.</p>
    <div class="project-detail-grid">
      <div><h3>What it does</h3><p>Provides an orchestration layer for managing the lifecycle and capacity of virtual machines across private cloud infrastructure.</p></div>
      <div><h3>How it ships</h3><p>ManageIQ and open source CloudForms components are combined with Target-specific automation and operational tooling.</p></div>
      <div><h3>Why it matters</h3><p>Together, Grove and VMaaS support more than 90% of workloads deployed on Target’s private cloud.</p></div>
    </div>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>Ruby</span><span>VMware</span><span>oVirt</span><span>Automation</span></div>
  </div>
